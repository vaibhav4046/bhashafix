# Code of Conduct

BhashaFix is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically rigorous
community across languages, regions, scripts, identities, and experience levels.

Contributors must communicate constructively, respect cultural and linguistic
context, accept evidence-based review, protect private data, and avoid
harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.

Project maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome; hostile behaviour is not.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will
be handled with discretion and without retaliation. This policy applies in
project repositories, discussions, events, and other spaces where a participant
represents BhashaFix.
